1. Introduction

Welcome to RMGC LIBRARY ("we", "us", "our", or "the App").

RMGC LIBRARY is an educational application designed to provide students with access to academic resources, including study notes, previous year question papers, syllabus information, subjects, semesters, college announcements, events, gallery content and other educational materials.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, processed and shared when you use RMGC LIBRARY.

2. Information We Collect

The information processed by RMGC LIBRARY depends on the features you use.

Account Information

RMGC LIBRARY uses Firebase Authentication to provide account registration and login.

  • Email address
  • Firebase user ID (UID)
  • Name, if provided
  • Profile information voluntarily provided by you
  • Academic stream/course, if provided
  • Semester, if provided
  • User role required for application functionality

3. Firebase Services

RMGC LIBRARY uses Google Firebase services to provide core application functionality.

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Storage
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging

These services may process information required to provide authentication, academic content, file storage, notifications and application functionality.

4. Push Notifications

RMGC LIBRARY may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver academic announcements, new study materials, examination updates, college events and other important notifications.

A Firebase Cloud Messaging token or similar app/device identifier may be processed to deliver notifications.

5. Academic Content

RMGC LIBRARY may provide study notes, previous year question papers, syllabus, semester resources, academic PDFs, timetables, announcements, events, gallery content and other educational resources.

6. PDF Files and Offline Storage

The App may allow users to view academic PDF files directly inside the application.

Certain files or application information may be cached locally to improve loading speed, reading performance and offline functionality where supported.

7. College Events and Gallery

RMGC LIBRARY may display college events, campus updates, gallery images, academic activities and other institutional content.

8. Advertising and Google AdMob

RMGC LIBRARY may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Advertising services may process information required to provide, measure, secure and improve advertising.

Depending on applicable settings and consent requirements, this may include advertising identifiers, device information, app information, IP address, approximate location derived from technical signals and advertisement interaction information.

RMGC LIBRARY does not sell users' personal information to advertisers.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate RMGC LIBRARY, including Google Firebase, Google Play services, Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google AdMob.

10. Account Deletion

Users who create an RMGC LIBRARY account may request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

In the application, users can use:

Profile → Settings → Delete Account

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through RMGC LIBRARY, including HTTPS/TLS encryption for supported network communications, Firebase Authentication, access controls and Firebase security mechanisms.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

RMGC LIBRARY is designed primarily for college and university students and is not specifically directed toward children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, services, data practices or legal requirements change.

The Last Updated date will be changed when material updates are made.
