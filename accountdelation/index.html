How to Request Account and Data Deletion

Method 1 — In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open the RMGC LIBRARY app.
  2. Go to Profile → Account Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.
Your account and associated personal information will be scheduled for deletion according to the app's deletion process.

Method 2 — Email Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request account and data deletion by email.

Email:
rajeshchoukhe6@gmail.com

Subject:
Account Deletion Request - RMGC Library

Request should include:
Your registered email address and full name.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, the following account-related personal information will be deleted where applicable:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Firebase Authentication account and UID
  • User profile information
  • Course / Department information
  • Semester information
  • Phone number
  • Push notification device tokens (FCM)

Data Retention

Upon receiving a valid email deletion request, associated personal data will be processed for deletion within 7 business days.

No personal account information is intentionally retained after the deletion process has been completed, except where retention is required by applicable law or for legitimate security purposes.
