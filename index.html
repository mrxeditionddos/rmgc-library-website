ABOUT RMGC LIBRARY

About RMGC Library

RMGC Library is a student-focused academic resource application designed to make educational materials easier to access from one place.

The application may provide study notes, previous-year question papers, syllabus information, semester and subject resources, college announcements, events, gallery content and educational PDF materials.

SECTION 01

Information We Collect

Account Information

When users register or sign in, the application may process information required for authentication and account management. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Firebase user ID
  • Academic stream or department when provided
  • Semester information when provided
  • Profile information voluntarily provided by the user

Technical Information

Technical information may be processed to provide application functionality, security, notifications and diagnostics.

  • Device and application identifiers
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging notification token
  • Operating system information
  • Crash and diagnostic information
SECTION 02

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate and manage user accounts.
  • To provide academic resources.
  • To provide relevant study materials.
  • To send academic announcements and notifications.
  • To maintain application security.
  • To diagnose technical problems.
  • To improve application performance.
SECTION 03

App Permissions

Permission Purpose
Internet Required to access online academic resources and application services.
Notifications Used to deliver academic announcements and important updates.
Photos / Media May be required for applicable profile, gallery or upload features.
Camera May be requested when an applicable feature requires image or document capture.

Users can manage applicable permissions through their Android device settings.

SECTION 04

Privacy & Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by RMGC Library.

The application uses Google Firebase services for authentication, database, storage and notification functionality where applicable.

Important: No online service can guarantee absolute security. We use reasonable safeguards, but absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
SECTION 05

Third-Party Services

RMGC Library may use Google and Firebase services to operate application functionality.

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Storage
  • Google Play Services where required

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

SECTION 06

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to data brokers.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate application functionality, including authentication, database, storage, notifications and infrastructure services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of users or the application.

SECTION 07

Data Retention

Account information may be retained while the account remains active or while information is reasonably necessary for providing services, security, legal compliance or legitimate operational requirements.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13 for purposes unrelated to the application's educational services.
