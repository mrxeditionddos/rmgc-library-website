SECTION 01

Introduction

Welcome to RMGC Library, an academic resources application designed to provide students with convenient access to educational materials and college-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how that information is used, how it is protected, and what choices users have regarding their information.

Application: RMGC Library
Package Name: com.rmgc.library
Developer Contact: rajeshchoukhe6@gmail.com
SECTION 02

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or use an account, the application may collect information necessary for authentication and account management, such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Firebase user identifier
  • Academic stream or department, when provided
  • Semester information, when provided
  • Profile information that you voluntarily provide

Technical Information

The application and its supporting services may process technical information required to operate the application, maintain security, deliver notifications, and diagnose technical problems.

  • Device or app identifiers
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging notification token
  • Operating system and app information
  • Crash and diagnostic information

Academic Content

RMGC Library provides academic resources such as notes, previous-year question papers, syllabus information, subject materials, announcements, and other educational content.

Academic resources made available through the application are not necessarily personal information about the user.

SECTION 03

How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To create and authenticate user accounts.
  • To maintain user sessions and account security.
  • To provide academic resources and personalized content.
  • To send academic announcements and important notifications, where the user has enabled notifications.
  • To maintain and improve application performance.
  • To investigate crashes, bugs, abuse, or security issues.
  • To provide administrative functionality to authorized administrators.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of RMGC Library unless required or permitted by applicable law.

SECTION 04

App Permissions

The application may request certain Android permissions depending on the features enabled in the application.

Permission / Access Purpose
Internet Required to retrieve academic content, account information, announcements and other online services.
Notifications Used to deliver academic announcements, updates and important notifications when enabled by the user.
Photos / Media May be requested for authorized profile, gallery or content-upload features where applicable.
Camera May be requested only if a feature requires capturing an image or document.

Permissions are requested only when required by a corresponding feature. Users can manage applicable permissions through Android device settings.

SECTION 05

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the application.

The application uses secure communication mechanisms provided by Google and Firebase services where applicable.

  • Data transmitted between the application and supported services is protected using encrypted network connections.
  • Firebase Authentication is used for account authentication where enabled.
  • Firebase Security Rules are used to control access to Firebase resources.
  • Administrative functions are intended to be restricted to authorized administrators.
No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Therefore, while reasonable safeguards are used, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

SECTION 07

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information to data brokers.

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate application functionality, such as authentication, database, storage, notifications, security and application infrastructure services.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or legal obligations.
  • Protect the security of the application.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse or unauthorized access.
  • Protect users, the institution or other persons from serious security or safety threats.
SECTION 08

Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or otherwise fulfill legitimate operational requirements.

Academic resources and institutional content may remain available while they are required for the operation of the academic portal.

SECTION 09

Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and applicable personal information.

Deletion Request

Send an account deletion request to:

Email: rajeshchoukhe6@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your RMGC Library account so that the request can be verified.

After verification, applicable account information will be deleted or anonymized, subject to information that we are required to retain for legal, security or operational reasons.

SECTION 10

Children's Privacy

RMGC Library is intended primarily for college and higher-education students and academic users.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13 years of age for purposes unrelated to the application's educational services.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.
